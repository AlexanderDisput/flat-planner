# Flat planner

Interactive 3D model of the attic flat. Drag furniture into place, turn it, check it fits under the roof slopes and measure distances.

Open it at: https://alexanderdisput.github.io/flat-planner/

- Everything runs in the browser; furniture positions are saved in your own browser only.
- The scale is estimated from the floor plan; correct it under "Scale check" with one real measurement.
